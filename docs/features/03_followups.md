# Follow-up engine
Queues owner reminders for leads still in `new` status at 24 hours, 3 days and 7 days. Updating status to contacted/qualified/won/lost stops reminders. No inbox integration exists: a customer reply alone cannot update status. Jobs recheck status, age, preferences and business scope before preparing mail. Overdue catch-up chooses only the latest reached stage; no burst of missed reminders. Leads older than 10 days are excluded. Drafts use the selected friendly/professional/concise voice and English/Hinglish, with deterministic fallback; only structured signals reach Gemini. Drafts are stored privately and included in owner reminder emails. Customers never receive an automatic follow-up.

Manual setup: migration 03 on the backup/restore copy, enable LEADFLOW_FOLLOWUPS_ENABLED only after email setup and test status cancellation. Owners choose preferences at /dashboard/settings. Configure a scheduler to GET /api/cron/automation with Authorization: Bearer CRON_SECRET every five minutes. A free existing Supabase pg_cron+pg_net/Vault schedule can do this after review; store URL and bearer token in Vault, never inline in committed SQL. Vercel Hobby daily cron is an alternative with up to a day of delay, not precise 24h reminders. No paid scheduler is provisioned. No redirects change.

Risks: delayed scheduler runs, owner forgetting to mark contacted, provider quota, model draft quality. Two leased jobs per invocation; 5-minute scheduling handles a small-business workload, not an unbounded bulk sender. Estimate at 1,000 monthly leads: up to 3,000 additional reminder emails and 3,000 optional model calls. Planning reserve ₹0–500 for capped AI usage plus email quota; paid email tier may be needed when notifications+reminders exceed free limits. These are budget estimates, not price quotes.

Rollback: disable LEADFLOW_FOLLOWUPS_ENABLED; keep drafts/jobs for inspection. Reverse migration 03 only on disposable staging, after reversing weekly report dependencies. Back up draft/history data before any approved removal.
