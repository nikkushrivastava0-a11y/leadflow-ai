- Data below is business-level public information only: no personal contact names, private emails, phone numbers, customer records or AI-inferred finances.
- Addresses/locations and official site presence may change; re-verify before any visit or outreach.

### Not priority for a first founder-led pilot

National-brand coaching centres such as Aakash, ALLEN and Physics Wallah have more formal operations and may require enterprise procurement or central approval. They can be studied as workflow benchmarks, but should not dominate the first 20 prospects.

## Qualification before outreach (do the research, do not assume)

For each prospective business, collect in a **private** tracking system:

1. Is this still an active, locally operated centre?
2. Is the publicly available contact path intended for business partnership queries rather than student support?
3. What exact evidence shows the centre accepts new admissions enquiries (e.g. public admissions/enquiry page)?
4. Who is the appropriate **business** decision maker, if publicly and voluntarily listed? Don't seek private personal details.
5. What can LeadFlow actually demonstrate today **after** Supabase and form security checks pass?
6. One neutral opening question, not an accusation about "lost leads."
7. Source URL and date checked; manual consent/response status.

## First experiment (20 contacts, not 20 auto messages)

- Start with 13 researched candidates above and find seven more through the same public-source, human-verified process.
- Personalise each message with one **verified** fact from their official site.
- Use business-appropriate channels; respect opt-outs, do not send bulk WhatsApp messages, and do not auto-contact anyone.
- Ask whether the admissions manager can spare ten minutes to explain the current workflow, **not** to purchase unverified features immediately.
- After 10 qualified conversations, group recurring pain points: missed follow-up, lack of ownership, poor reporting, or no problem.
- If replies are low, adjust audience and offer; do not automatically increase message volume.
